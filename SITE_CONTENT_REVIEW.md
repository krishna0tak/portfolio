## 02. CHAPTER 01 & 02: THE DESIGN BRIEF, ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEM & 8-PHASE PROCESS
[File: src/components/HeroSection.jsx]

Section Badge:
01. THE DESIGN BRIEF & ARCHITECTURAL PROBLEM

Main Headline:
Engineering Tactile Luxury Through Zero-Glue Structural Origami.

Project Context Paragraph 1:
An exhaustive packaging architecture and industrial design case study by Vidushi Mewara. The core mandate was to conceptualize and engineer a cohesive 11-SKU identity for CAMEÓLA Paris—a clean beauty house dedicated to botanical purity without synthetic fragrance.

Design Challenge & Solution Paragraph 2:
Rather than defaulting to standard composite plastics and non-recyclable hot-melt adhesives typical in luxury cosmetics, this project rethinks the physical unboxing ritual entirely through sustainable FSC virgin paperboard, friction-fit locking geometry, and classical French typographic restraint.

Project Hashtags / Meta Tags:
- #IndustrialPackaging
- #ZeroGlueDieline
- #FSCPaperboard
- #FrenchAestheticHeritage
- #3DKinematicStudy
- #MaterialTesting

### Software & Digital Production Tools:

- Version / Engine: Blender 3.6 LTS / Cycles Engine
- Status Badge: Primary 3D Pipeline (Live Simulation)
- Technical Highlight: 299-Frame Unboxing Kinematics
- Description: Engineered the 3D container geometry, folding carton opening kinematics, and procedural material shaders. Leveraged Cycles ray-tracing with subsurface scattering (SSS) for cosmetic emulsions and calibrated Fresnel IOR for glass and acrylic specular behavior.
- Core Technical Skills:
  1. Kinematic Box Opening Rigging
  2. Subsurface Light Scattering (SSS)
  3. Specular Roughness & IOR Physics
  4. Photorealistic Studio Lighting Setup
  5. 299-Frame Scroll Web Sequence

Tool 2: Adobe Illustrator
- Category: Vector CAD & Dieline Engineering
- Version: Vector CAD & Print Separation
- Status Badge: 1:1 Production Blueprint
- Technical Highlight: 11 Full Dieline Architectures
- Description: Drafted millimeter-exact vector packaging dielines with distinct line layers for cut, score, and perforation. Built dedicated separation plates for spot UV varnish, blind debossing, and hot rose-gold foil stamping with 0.1mm press registration tolerances.
- Core Technical Skills:
  1. 1:1 CAD Dieline Geometry
  2. Spot UV & Foil Stamping Separation
  3. Cut, Score & Perforation Layering

Discipline 1:
- Icon/Field: Zero-Glue Structural Origami
- Subtitle: Sustainable Packaging Mechanics

- Icon/Field: Color Systems & Press Feasibility
